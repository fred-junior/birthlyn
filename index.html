Bervelyn's "Masterpiece" Gari Lasagna

Ingredients
  • A dangerous amount of Gari
  • Lukewarm water
  • Pinch of salt
  • Carrots
  • Green pepper
  • Sausage
  • Vegetable oil
  • Avocado 🥑
Instructions
  1. So you pour a little water on the gari and mix to soften it.
  2. Then you put a pan on fire with oil.
  3. Pour the cut vegetables into it.
  4. Add the sausage and stir for a bit.
  5. Stir add a little spice that’s to your satisfaction.
  6. Then you add the gari and stir.
  7. More stirring😌😌😌.
  8. You can prepare stew if you want then you add any protein of your choice.
  9. Garnish with the avocado
  10. Enjoy! Keep the fire extinguisher nearby just in case[Her recipe is not really that safe🤣🤣🤣].
